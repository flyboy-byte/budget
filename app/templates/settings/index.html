Minimum time between "Sync now" calls per bank connection (also applies to the daily cron sync). Default 360 (6 hours); minimum 5.
This dropdown/toggle changes your "cash on hand" by calculating SimpleFIN's output a different way. Off (default): balances come from your bank's posted/ledger total, same as always. On: for accounts (not debts/loans), balances come from your bank's "available" total instead, which subtracts pending holds/charges your bank hasn't posted yet — so it can be a bit lower (or, for overdrafts, more negative) than the ledger total. Only takes effect on the next sync.
Off (default): every synced balance sits on the Bank Sync review screen until you manually apply it — safe, but easy to forget, and safe-to-spend won't move until you do. On: if a newly-synced balance is within the amount below of what's currently stored, it's applied automatically, no review needed. A bigger jump than that (a real balance swing, or a bad read from your bank) still waits for your manual review either way, so this can't silently blow past a real surprise.
How big a balance change still counts as "small" for the auto-apply toggle above. Default $250.
